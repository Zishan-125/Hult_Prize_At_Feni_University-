About Us

A Historic First
For The Feni Region

Hult Prize at Feni University is the inaugural On-Campus Program of the world's largest student social entrepreneurship competition in the Feni region. We empower university students to build innovative, for-profit startups that tackle global challenges aligned with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

Named one of the top five ideas changing the world by President Bill Clinton and TIME Magazine, the Hult Prize is often called the "Nobel Prize for Students." Our campus program serves as the first step of a transformative journey — from local champion to the global stage in London.

Competition Journey

The Road To $1 Million

Five progressive phases take you from your campus to the Global Finals in London.

🏫

On-Campus Program

Register your team of 2–4 students, develop your social enterprise idea, and compete in the local campus competition.

September – January
🇧🇩

National Competition

Campus winners advance to pitch at national events. Team Biz Venture represented Feni University at KUET, Khulna!

March – May
💻

Digital Incubator

National winners access world-class mentors to validate product-market fit, build MVPs, and develop go-to-market strategies.

May – July
🏰

Global Accelerator

Top startups spend a month at the historic Ashridge House near London for intensive mentorship, workshops, and investor pitches.

July – August
🌟

Global Finals

Six finalists pitch to distinguished judges for the $1 million USD Hult Prize — the world's largest student social enterprise award.
